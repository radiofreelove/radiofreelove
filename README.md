# radiofreelove

Two free, plain-language guides. Each is independent, and each shows the official source behind every fact it states.

## The Stasis Project

A guide for people applying for the Oregon Health Plan (OHP) under the 2027 work or activity rules.

- Site: https://projectstasis.pages.dev
- Source: https://github.com/radiofreelove/projectstasis

Not run by the State of Oregon, the Oregon Health Authority, or the Oregon Department of Human Services.

## The Strand Project

A guide to changing your legal name and gender marker in Oregon, Washington, and Utah, step by step and in the right order.

- Site: https://projectstrand.pages.dev
- Source: https://github.com/radiofreelove/projectstrand

Not run by, or connected to, any court or government agency.

## Report a mistake

Open an issue on the project's own repository and pick a template. Issues are public, and so is your GitHub account name. Do not include personal details about your own case.
